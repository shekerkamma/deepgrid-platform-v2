Voice: bm_george (Kokoro, local) · target 140-155 spoken wpm

## 1 — One loop
A humanoid robot has to see, decide and move in one continuous loop, fast enough to work beside people. This is that loop on Deepgrid's humanoid platform.

## 2 — Perception
It starts with perception. Several sensors are fused into one three-dimensional picture of the room, objects are recognised, and depth is measured in real time, thirty frames every second.

## 3 — Interpretation
Interpretation turns that picture into meaning. The system reasons about context, decides what matters, and understands a command spoken in plain language.

## 4 — Planning
Planning comes next. The task is broken into steps, and each motion is planned before a single joint moves.

## 5 — Action
Then action. Motor control drives seven degrees of freedom with sub-millimetre precision, while motion-control AI keeps the robot balanced as it walks. An operator can take over remotely, and the platform keeps learning from real-world video.

## 6 — Close
Perception, interpretation, planning, action. One pipeline, from silicon to motion. What you have seen is an illustration of that pipeline, not recorded footage.
